# Contrasting examples

Read when choosing between patterns or checking whether an attractive idea has become an automatic recommendation. These are hypothetical examples, not case studies with measured outcomes. Transfer the reasoning and adapt the design.

## One mechanism, different useful expressions

| Context | Desired change and emotional direction | Possible design | Context-specific judgment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Language practice | From uncertainty to participation and pride | Offer a supported exchange, then a chance to respond with less help; recognize the actual accomplishment | Increasing independence can be meaningful progress; one successful exercise does not establish durable mastery |
| Habit tracking | From vague intention to a workable routine and follow-through | Build a personal cue/action plan, acknowledge reported activity, and make adjusting the routine easy | Value may happen away from the app; a stable action can stay the same, and fewer app visits may accompany success |
| Occasional reservation | From uncertainty to confidence and anticipation | Make availability, the total commitment, useful comparisons, and the next step clear | Completion and reliable fulfilment matter; a daily return loop may have no purpose |
| Team workspace | From individual setup to shared usefulness and belonging | Let the creator prepare a useful shared object; give invitees a clear contribution and acknowledgement | Creator signup is different from team activation; each role needs a reason to act, and access requirements can be essential |
| Creative tool | From blank-canvas hesitation to authorship and pride | Provide an editable starting point and a fast preview of the person's own result | Defaults and personal investment can work together; importing an existing work may be more valuable than a beginner tutorial |

## Timing a commitment

**Interactive value is immediately available.** A creative tool might let someone edit a starter and preview the result before offering an account to sync projects. “Save this project across devices” connects the account to an experienced benefit.

**The useful outcome comes later.** A routine planner might first produce a workable schedule. Saving and coordinating that plan can justify an account before the offline activity occurs. Track the planned action separately from later reported completion.

**Access is part of the task.** A private shared workspace may need sign-in before displaying actual content. A clear invitation, who is inviting whom, the intended contribution, and an accurate preview can make that commitment understandable. Do not add a mandatory tutorial merely to imitate value-first onboarding.

## What progress can mean

An exercise can show declining reliance on hints. A reservation can show a completed transaction and a confirmed next step. A team can show contributions resolved into a shared decision. A routine can show repeated actions and successful adaptation. Choose the representation after deciding what change matters; do not reuse a percentage, streak, or difficulty ladder by default.

Likewise, ending an experience can be valuable. A clear completion state can create confidence and closure; an invitation to continue should have a relevant purpose. A product used once a year needs a different retention interpretation from daily practice.

## Scale the output

**Small copy request:** Provide the requested text and, if helpful, a brief reason. For a reminder tied to a user's chosen plan, a specific next action may be enough. Do not introduce account flows, research plans, or a new feature set.

**Focused flow audit:** Locate the consequential issue or opportunity, propose actual copy and state changes, explain the intended effect and a material tradeoff, and suggest a proportionate check. Preserve useful parts of the flow.

**New feature design:** Shape the main action, feedback, relevant alternate states, and the reason for any next commitment. When implementation is requested, translate those decisions into the project's existing components and conventions. An opportunity map is useful only if it advances that work.
